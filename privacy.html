Cropshoot Privacy Policy

Cropshoot does not collect, store, or share any personal information, and the app has no servers of its own. It works on your device. Photos you select are used only to create your edited images, and the app never uploads them or sends them to us or to any third party. To make editing easier, the app keeps copies of your three most recent drafts in its own storage on your phone. They stay on your device and are removed when you delete the app. Because they are part of the app's data, they may be included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or your computer), which Apple manages under your own settings. Cropshoot asks for permission to add to your Photos library only when you save finished images and albums. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. When you tap Post now, or when the app opens another app for you, your finished images are handed to the app you choose, and that app's own privacy policy applies. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page. Questions: gunars@gunars.com.
